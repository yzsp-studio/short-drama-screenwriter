## 🇨🇳 中文

微短剧编剧助手，引导用户从选题一步步完成 50–100 集完整剧本创作，支持国内竖屏短剧与海外 ReelShort、DramaBox 格式。内置爆款方法论：黄金 5 秒开场规则、五大钩子类型、冲突节点编排、单集 1.5–3 分钟三段式节奏曲线、五大爽感矩阵、四阶反派金字塔与付费点设计，用悬念结构支撑观众留存与完播率。产物写入项目目录下的 .short-drama/{剧名}/ 文件夹并导出创作方案、角色档案、分集目录、分集剧本与合规报告，进度用 .drama-state.json 持久化；每阶段完成后与用户确认再进入下一阶段，用户可在任一阶段要求返回修改。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 从选题 / 题材出发 → 创作方案、角色档案、分集目录、分集剧本、合规报告与最终导出
- 黄金 5 秒开场规则 + 前 30 秒三要素，开场直接制造即时冲突或强烈悬念
- 五大钩子类型与单集 1.5–3 分钟三段式节奏曲线，控制每集悬念落点与留存
- 五大爽感矩阵 + 四阶反派金字塔（T4 隐藏 Boss 终极反转）搭建冲突与打脸节奏
- 付费点三大原则：设在情绪峰值与悬念未解处，而非平淡叙事中
- 内容合规检查清单先过一遍红线内容再交付，避免上线风险

## 🇬🇧 English

A micro-drama screenwriting assistant that walks from topic selection to a complete 50–100 episode script, supporting domestic vertical short dramas and overseas ReelShort or DramaBox formats. It embeds a proven hit methodology: a golden 5-second opening rule, five hook types, conflict-beat arrangement, a 1.5–3 minute three-part pacing curve per episode, a five-type satisfaction matrix, a four-tier villain pyramid and paywall-point design, all structured to hold retention and completion. Outputs land in a .short-drama/{title}/ project folder as a creative plan, character profiles, episode directory, per-episode scripts, a compliance report and a final export, with progress persisted in .drama-state.json. Each stage is confirmed with the user before the next begins, and any stage can be reopened for revision.

### ✨ What it does

- Topic or genre in → creative plan, character profiles, episode directory, per-episode scripts, compliance report and final export
- Golden 5-second opening rule plus the first-30-seconds three elements, opening on immediate conflict or strong suspense
- Five hook types with a 1.5–3 minute three-part pacing curve per episode to place suspense beats and hold retention
- Five-type satisfaction matrix plus a four-tier villain pyramid (T4 hidden boss, the final reversal) to build conflict and payoff rhythm
- Three paywall principles: place the pay point at the emotional peak and on an unresolved suspense, never in flat narration
- Runs a content-compliance checklist over red-line material before delivery to avoid launch risk
